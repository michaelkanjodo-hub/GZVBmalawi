🏠 HOME PAGE

Your Dashboard

1

Live Countdown & Score Pad

The home page shows a live countdown to your next match, a scrolling ticker with recent results, and an interactive score pad to track games in real-time. Use the + and − buttons to update scores, and save completed matches to your history.

2

Highlights Reel

Watch curated volleyball highlights from YouTube, or see live streams from your community. The reel auto-rotates through featured clips every 45 seconds. Click any highlight to watch it full-screen.

3

Court Map & Leaderboard

See practice locations pinned on an interactive court map. The leaderboard shows top players ranked by total skill points. Register your team to get on the board!

📡 LIVE STREAMING

Go Live & Watch

4

Start a Camera Stream

Click the 📡 button (bottom right) on any page. Choose "Stream from my camera" and allow camera access. You'll get a watch link to share via WhatsApp, SMS, or chat. The other person just opens the link — no codes needed!

5

Watch a Live Stream

When someone shares a watch link, just open it. The stream plays automatically using PeerJS technology. You'll see the broadcaster's camera feed in real-time with low latency.

6

External Streams

You can also paste a YouTube Live, Twitch, or Facebook Live URL to embed external streams. Viewers watch directly on the website.

🎬 VIDEOS PAGE

Upload & Watch

7

Upload Your Videos

Go to the Videos page and drag & drop your video file (MP4, WebM, MOV up to 100MB). Add a title, category (Highlights, Training, Matches, Skills, Fun), description, and your name. Click Upload and it appears in the gallery instantly!

8

Browse & Filter

Watch videos from the community. Use category filters to find specific types. Hover over thumbnails to preview. Click to play in full-screen. Share videos with friends using the share button.

📝 REGISTRATION

Join the Community

9

Player Registration

Register as a player with your name, photo, position, skills, emergency contact, and medical info. Sign the liability waiver digitally. Your profile appears on the leaderboard and team roster.

10

Team Registration

Create a team with a name, logo, city, coach, and contact info. Add members from registered players. Your team appears on the Teams page and can book matches.

🔄 SYNC

Cross-Device Sync

11

Real-Time Sync via Supabase

Everything syncs across all devices in real-time! Register a player on your phone, and it appears on your computer instantly. Match scores, donations, live streams, and chat messages all sync automatically. No page refresh needed.

12

How It Works

The website uses Supabase for real-time database sync. When anyone adds or updates data, it's pushed to Supabase. All other devices receive the update instantly via WebSocket subscriptions. Data is also saved locally in localStorage as a backup.
